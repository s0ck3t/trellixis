Quick Admin Setup

  1. Open Jira Settings → Apps → VaultGuard Compliance Console.
  2. Choose Auto-Clean (overwrites secrets automatically) or untick it for Monitor-Only testing.
  3. Use 3. Safe Lists to ignore internal company email domains or office VPN IP ranges.
  4. Use 4. Scan Existing Tickets to run a background JQL scan over older issues.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Does VaultGuard delete Jira Issue History? Comments and original attachments are wiped cleanly, while Jira Cloud locks issue field changelogs against all apps. For strict GDPR erasure on a field edit, clone the cleaned issue and delete the original.
  • Why did a ticket not update? Check 1. Rules & Actions to ensure Auto-Clean is ticked and the project is not listed under Projects to Skip.
