VaultGuard Zero-Egress Compliance for Jira Cloud

Built by Trellixis, VaultGuard automatically spots and redacts credit cards, personal identifiers, passwords, and API keys inside Jira tickets, comments, Smart Links, and uploaded config files, without sending a single byte outside Atlassian Cloud.

30 Built-In Detectors + Custom Rules

Covers payment cards (Luhn), IBANs (MOD-97), national & healthcare IDs (SSN, NHS, NINO, SIN, DNI/NIE, INSEE, Steuer-ID), Atlassian/AWS/GitHub/GitLab/Vault/Stripe/OpenAI keys, and assigned password= / ?token= secrets.

Jira-Wide Coverage

Scans Summary, Description, Environment, Smart Link URLs, Jira & JSM comments, custom text fields, and uploaded files (.env.*, .pem, .key, .ppk, .tfvars, .sh, .log, .json, .csv, .yaml).

Tamper-Evident Audit Log

Saves one-way SHA-256 receipts instead of raw secrets. Export CSV/JSON reports for GDPR, HIPAA, DORA, PCI-DSS v4.0, ISO 27001, and SOC 2 audits, with built-in DSAR cleanup.
